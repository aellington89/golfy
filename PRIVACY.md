# Golfy privacy policy

*Last updated 28 October 2026. This policy covers Golfy for Android and for
Windows.*

## The short version

Golfy collects nothing. Everything you enter stays on your device. Golfy has
no account, no ads and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet.
Nothing leaves your device unless you send it somewhere yourself.

## What Golfy keeps, and where

Golfy stores what you enter in one database on your device: courses, tee and
pin sets and their yards, rounds, holes, shots and events.

- **Android:** the database is in Golfy's private app storage, which other
  apps can't read. Uninstalling Golfy deletes it.
- **Windows:** the database is one file, `golfy.sqlite`, in your Documents
  folder.

Golfy keeps nothing else about you. It has no name, email, location, contacts
or device identifiers, and no record of how you use the app.

## What leaves your device

Nothing leaves on its own. Golfy contains no code that connects to the
internet. On Android it couldn't anyway: Golfy has no permission to use the
internet, and it asks for no other access either, including storage, location,
camera and contacts.

Something leaves your device only when you send it:

- **Backups** (Settings → Back up your data).
  - A backup is one file. It holds your golf data, the version of Golfy that
    made it, when it was made, and whether it was made on Android or Windows.
    It contains no device identifiers.
  - It goes only where you send it, through Android's share sheet or the
    Windows Save dialog.
  - On Android, your latest backup stays in Golfy's private cache until you
    make the next one. That lets the app you shared it with finish reading it.
- **Restoring a backup** (Settings → Restore from a backup). Golfy reads only
  the file you pick. On Android, the system gives Golfy a temporary copy of
  that file, which Golfy deletes once it has read it.
- **Links** (Settings → About). These open in your browser, never inside
  Golfy. Once a page is open, the site's own privacy policy applies; for this
  policy and for Golfy's issue tracker, that's GitHub's.

## Help & feedback

**Help & feedback** opens a new issue on Golfy's GitHub page, in your browser.
Your Golfy version and your platform (Android or Windows) are already filled
in. Nothing is sent until you submit the issue, and you can change or delete
anything in it first.

Issues on GitHub are **public**, so leave out anything you don't want
published.

## Analytics, advertising and third parties

Golfy has none of these. It contains no analytics, crash reporting or
advertising code, and it shares nothing with anyone.

Golfy is built with open-source packages, which are listed with their licenses
under Settings → About → Open-source licenses. None of them collects or sends
data.

## Children

Golfy collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

- **Android:** uninstall Golfy, or clear its storage in Android's settings.
- **Windows:** delete `golfy.sqlite` from your Documents folder.

Your backups are files you control: delete them wherever you keep them.
